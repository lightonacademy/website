FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can enroll?

Anyone ready to learn: children and teens aged 12–17 (Innovators Bootcamp), recent high school graduates, university students, professionals and adults upgrading their skills.

I’m a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Level 1 and Your Next Step need no experience, and every class is taught step by step.

Are classes online or onsite?

Both. Most programs run onsite at our training centers in Paynesville, Chicken Soup, Duazon and Sinkor. The Innovators Bootcamp and some courses run fully online.

Do class days and times change?

Yes. Schedules change from intake to intake. Each program page shows the current days and times, and we confirm your class time on WhatsApp when you register.

Do I need my own laptop?

A laptop helps, but it isn’t always required. Online programs like the Innovators Bootcamp work on a smartphone. Ask us about the program you’re interested in.

How do I pay?

Most programs can be paid in two parts: 50% before training starts and 50% in week 2. After you register, we’ll WhatsApp you the payment details.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every program ends with a certificate of completion.
