FAQ

Questions about free lessons

Is it really free?

Yes. Every lesson, practice exercise, quiz and certificate on LightOn Learn is free. You only pay if you choose to join one of our live bootcamps.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your progress is saved in the browser on your phone or computer. Use the same device and browser to keep your progress.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. Every lesson works on a smartphone. For web development a laptop is easier, but you can still edit and run code on a phone.

What is the difference between free lessons and a bootcamp?

Free lessons teach the basics at your own pace. A bootcamp adds a live trainer, classmates, bigger projects, feedback and a LightOn Academy certificate.
