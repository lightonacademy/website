About LightOn Academy

Lighting up Africa through digital skills

LightOn Academy is a Liberian digital skills and capacity development institution. We equip learners of all ages, from children to working professionals, with practical technology skills to work smarter, earn better and grow.

Our story

More than a training center: a social mission

We work in underserved communities across Liberia, equipping ordinary Liberians with digital skills that open doors to employment, entrepreneurship and economic independence.

Affordable fees, installment payment plans and community-based training centers in Paynesville, Chicken Soup, Duazon and Sinkor remove the barriers that usually keep low-income learners from quality digital education. Our youth programs invest in the next generation of African innovators, coders and leaders.

“LightOn me, LightOn you, LightOn Africa.”

Mission

Bridge the digital skills gap in Liberia

We offer accessible, affordable and practical technology education, because today’s investment in skills is tomorrow’s reward.

Vision

Light up Africa

We aim to light up Africa through digital transformation and capacity development, raising a generation of skilled, innovative and self-sufficient Africans.
