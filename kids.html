Why this bootcamp exists

What does your child do during the holidays?

For many parents, the holidays bring a quiet worry. Without something meaningful to hold their attention, even a responsible child can drift into passive screen time or the wrong company.

  • Stops learning loss: they keep the sharpness they built during the school year.
  • Turns screen time into building time: they create things instead of only scrolling.
  • In caring hands: every live session is structured and supervised, and every group is monitored.
  • Values with skills: a leadership track nurtures good character.

Questions parents ask

Common questions

Does my child need a laptop?

No. Everything works on a smartphone with free tools and a browser. Woodwork uses materials found locally, so there’s nothing to buy.

My child did this bootcamp before. Is it the same thing again?

No. Returning students join as Builders, with a stretch challenge in every subject, so they go deeper into real projects instead of repeating the basics.

Is it really free?

Yes, the training is completely free. A one-time $40 registration fee secures a spot and covers the full two months. There are no hidden costs.

What if we can’t find wood materials?

Any scrap, broken or small piece of wood works. Facilitators help students who need help getting started.

How do I register?
